# Quiko - Project Context

**Tagline**: "Just Quick As That"

## Project Overview

Quiko is a peer-to-peer package delivery platform that transforms every travel journey into a delivery opportunity. The platform connects users who need to send packages with travelers going on the same route, enabling faster, cheaper, and more sustainable deliveries.

### Core Value Proposition

- **For Senders**: Cost-effective, fast delivery leveraging existing travel routes
- **For Travelers**: Earn extra income during trips they're already taking
- **For Environment**: Reduced carbon footprint through shared mobility
- **For Business**: Efficient logistics without additional resource consumption

---

## Mission, Vision & Goal

**Goal**: Race against the clock to swiftly transfer people's needs, ensuring instant fulfillment because every moment matters when you're turning trips into tips.

**Mission**: Pioneer sustainable deliveries, championing eco-friendly practices that leave no trace, ensuring our planet thrives with each package we transport.

**Vision**: Ignite a movement where every sender today transforms into tomorrow's traveler, and every receiver becomes the sender of a sustainable tomorrow. Quiko aims to sculpt a future where our united efforts create a world free from pollutants, one delivery at a time.

## Problem Statement

### Customer Pain Points

1. **Air Transport**: Unpredictable price variations causing budgeting challenges (~75% affected)
2. **Road Transport**: Unexpectedly long delivery times causing frustration (~70% affected)
3. **Rail Transport**: Inconvenient and time-consuming delivery management (~50% affected)

### Business Pain Points

- Delays in priority parcel shipments demanding faster, cheaper delivery without extra resource consumption

### Market Research Findings (50 respondents)

- **54%** prefer nearby pick-up for lower price and faster delivery
- **18-30 age group**: Strongest preference for nearby pickup
- **31-40 age group**: Favor door delivery
- Priority factors: Cost-effectiveness and speed over pure convenience

---

## The Quiko Model

### How It Works (Game Plan)

1. **Traveler beginning journey** → Point A (Origin)
2. **Sender waiting with parcel** → Point A (Pickup)
3. **Receiver waiting for parcel** → Point B (Destination)
4. **Traveler's final destination** → Completes journey

### Key Differentiators vs Traditional Courier

**Traditional Courier**: Sender → Drop-off point → Multiple transport modes (plane/truck/bus/train) → Multiple transfers → Warehouse → Road vehicle → Final delivery

**Quiko**: Direct handoff from Sender → Traveler → Receiver (minimal handovers, faster delivery)

**Calculation**:

## Customer Journey

### Registration & Onboarding

1. **Login/Register screen**
2. **Registration**: Name, Contact, Email address, Password (create + confirm)
3. **KYC Gateway**: Terms and conditions acceptance
4. **Login confirmation**
5. **Role selection**: Send or Travel

### Sender Flow

1. **Create Sender Profile**:
   - From location
   - To location
   - Date
   - Item description
   - Weight
   - Dimensions
   - Price quote
   - Description

2. **Check for Travelers**: System displays available travelers on route

3. **Match Found**: "It's a match" screen

4. **Communication**: Chat with traveler
   - Location sharing
   - Photo verification
   - Terms confirmation

5. **Tracking**: Map view with partner details (1-4 partners shown)

6. **Product Verification**: Photo upload by traveler

7. **Delivery Confirmation**: Receiver enters OTP

8. **Payment**: Process payment

9. **Feedback**: Rate and comment

### Traveler Flow

1. **Create Traveler Profile**:
   - From location
   - To location
   - Departure time/place
   - Arrival time/place
   - Quoted price (max price can quote)

2. **Check for Senders**: System displays available delivery requests

3. **Match Found**: "It's a match" screen

4. **Communication**: Chat with sender
   - Coordinate pickup
   - Verify package details

5. **Pickup**: Get partner details with map

6. **Product Verification**: Upload photo, accept terms

7. **Get Receiver Details**: Contact info and delivery location

8. **Delivery**: Receiver verification via OTP

9. **Payment Received**: Confirm successful delivery

10. **Feedback**: Rate and comment

### Receiver Flow

1. **Notification**: SMS/Push notification about incoming package
2. **Tracking**: Can track package location
3. **Delivery**: Meet traveler, verify with OTP
4. **Confirmation**: Package received successfully
