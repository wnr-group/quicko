## 🎯 The Big Idea (30-Second Pitch)

**"Quiko turns travelers into delivery heroes."**

Every day, millions of people travel between cities with empty luggage space. Meanwhile, millions more need to send packages but can't afford expensive couriers. Quiko connects them - travelers earn money, senders save money, and packages arrive faster than any traditional courier.

**It's like Uber, but for package delivery. It's like Airbnb, but for luggage space.**

## 💬 Brand Voice & Messaging

### Voice Characteristics
1. **Friendly but Professional**: "Hey, we got this" not "Dear valued customer"
2. **Confident**: "Your package will arrive" not "We hope it arrives"
3. **Transparent**: "Here's exactly how it works" not vague promises
4. **Community-Driven**: "We're a network of real people" not "We're a company"
5. **Optimistic**: "Turn your trip into earnings" not "Offset your travel costs"

### Do's and Don'ts

**Do**:
- Use conversational language ("Let's match you with a traveler")
- Emphasize speed and savings ("50-80% cheaper, 2x faster")
- Highlight trust ("Verified travelers, secure payment")
- Show personality (emojis in app, not in legal docs)

**Don't**:
- Use corporate jargon ("leverage synergies", "disrupt the ecosystem")
- Over-promise ("guaranteed delivery" - we can't control everything)
- Minimize risks ("nothing can go wrong" - be honest about edge cases)
- Compare negatively to competitors (focus on our benefits)

## 🎓 Brand Guidelines (Quick Reference)

### Logo Usage
✅ **Do**:
- Use official logo files (vector format)
- Maintain clear space (minimum 20px padding)
- Use on white or black backgrounds

❌ **Don't**:
- Stretch, distort, or rotate logo
- Change colors (only yellow + black versions)
- Place on busy backgrounds (logo must be visible)

### Color Usage
✅ **Do**:
- Yellow for CTAs, highlights, energy
- Black for text, professionalism, trust
- White for backgrounds, breathing room

❌ **Don't**:
- Use yellow text on white (poor contrast)
- Overuse yellow (should be 20% of screen max)
- Introduce new brand colors without approval

### Typography
✅ **Do**:
- Use system fonts (SF Pro, Roboto)
- Bold for headings, regular for body
- Minimum 16px for body text (readability)

❌ **Don't**:
- Use decorative fonts (comic sans, papyrus, etc.)
- Go below 14px (accessibility issue)
- Use more than 2 font weights per screen

### Imagery
✅ **Do**:
- Use real photos (travelers, packages, people)
- Diverse representation (age, gender, ethnicity)
- Bright, energetic, optimistic tones

❌ **Don't**:
- Use stock photos (overly staged, fake)
- Dark, moody, negative imagery
- Logos of competitors in frame
